Explain the relationship between liquidity and solvency. In your answer, describe how a company can be liquid but insolvent, and vice versa.
Grading criteria — visible to teacher only
Award 2 pts for correctly distinguishing between liquidity (short-term ability to meet obligations) and solvency (long-term ability). Award 2 pts for a valid example of a liquid-but-insolvent company (e.g. bridge financing). Award 2 pts for the inverse example with clear reasoning.
